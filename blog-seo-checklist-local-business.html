SEO Checklist for Local Business Websites

SEO · 5 min read

Most local business sites don't need advanced SEO — they need the basics done correctly. This is the checklist worth running through on every project before you call it done, whether it's your client's site or your own.

On-page basics

  • Title tag includes the service and the city. "Home" as a page title helps no one — "Interior Designer in Jaipur | [Business Name]" tells Google and the searcher exactly what the page is.
  • One clear H1 per page that matches the search intent, not a slogan.
  • Meta description written for humans, not stuffed with keywords — it's what shows up in the search snippet and affects click-through rate.
  • Page loads fast on mobile. Most local searches happen on a phone; a slow site loses visitors before they see anything.
  • HTTPS enabled. Browsers flag non-HTTPS sites as "not secure," which kills trust instantly.

Local-specific essentials

  • NAP consistency — Name, Address, Phone number should match exactly across the website, Google Business Profile, and any directory listings. Mismatches confuse both users and Google.
  • Google Business Profile claimed and complete — category, hours, photos, and a link back to the website. For most local searches, this matters more than the website itself.
  • Embedded map or clear address on the contact page, not buried in a PDF or image.
  • Click-to-call and click-to-WhatsApp buttons on mobile — remove friction between "found you" and "contacted you."

Content that actually helps

  • A services page for each major offering, not one page trying to cover everything.
  • Real photos of the business, not stock images — they build trust and differentiate from competitors using generic templates.
  • Reviews or testimonials, even just 2–3, visible on the homepage.

What to skip for a first version

Blog content, schema markup beyond the basics, and backlink building all matter eventually, but they're not what determines whether a first-time local business website works. Get the fundamentals above right first — they're most of the impact for a fraction of the effort.
