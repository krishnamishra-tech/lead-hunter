Frequently asked

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free plan works immediately after signup — no card required.

What exactly is OpenStreetMap search?

It's a second, independent source of business data — useful for storefronts and offices like cafes, salons, clinics, and shops. It's a Pro-only feature because it pulls from a different, more resource-intensive data source than the free Auto-search.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — Pro is billed monthly or yearly with no lock-in. If you cancel, you keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.

Is there a team plan?

Pro includes up to 2 additional teammate seats at no extra cost — invite them from Settings once you're on Pro.
