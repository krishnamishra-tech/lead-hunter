How to Find Real Estate Leads in Any Indian City

Lead Generation · 5 min read

If you sell to real estate agents — website design, digital marketing, photography, staging, CRM software — your first challenge in any new city is the same: who actually exists here, and how do you reach them?

1. Start with a city and a radius, not a whole state

Searching "real estate agents in Uttar Pradesh" returns noise. Searching "real estate agents in Gomti Nagar, Lucknow" returns a workable list. Narrow to a locality first, then expand once you've worked through it.

2. Pull from more than one source

No single directory has complete coverage. Google Maps tends to have the most active listings with reviews; JustDial and 99acres often surface smaller agencies that haven't invested in their Google presence; OpenStreetMap sometimes has offices neither of the others lists. Cross-referencing two or three sources catches the businesses that would otherwise get missed entirely.

3. Prioritize by what's missing, not what's present

A real estate agency with no website and no listed hours is a warmer lead than one with a polished site and thousands of reviews — the first one has an obvious, unsolved problem you can point to. Sort your list by "no website" or "few photos" before you start reaching out.

4. Get the phone number, then move to WhatsApp immediately

Email response rates for local businesses in India are low. A short WhatsApp message, sent from a real (not bulk-looking) number, gets seen and answered far more often. Keep the first message under three sentences.

5. Track everything from lead one

It's tempting to skip organizing your list until you have "enough" leads. Don't — by lead 15 you won't remember who you already messaged. A simple status column (New → Messaged → Follow-up → Replied) prevents duplicate outreach and missed follow-ups.

Doing this manually vs. with a tool

All five steps above can be done by hand with a spreadsheet and a few browser tabs — it just takes time.
