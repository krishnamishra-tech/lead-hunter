About LocalScout

Who we are

LocalScout is built for web design freelancers and small agencies who spend too much time finding clients and not enough time doing the work they're good at. We help you find local businesses that don't have a real website yet, show them an instant preview of what one could look like, and get you talking to them on WhatsApp — all before you've written a single cold email.

Alongside the core app, we publish a set of free tools — a website audit checker, invoice generator, review response generator, QR code generator, and more — built for the same freelancers and agencies, whether or not they ever use the paid product.
