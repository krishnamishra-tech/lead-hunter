The Cold Pitch Script That Gets Local Businesses to Say Yes

Outreach · 5 min read

Most cold pitches to local businesses fail for the same reason: they open with what the sender does, not what's wrong with the business right now. "Hi, I build websites for local businesses" gets ignored because it's generic — it could be sent to anyone. Here's a structure that leads with something specific instead.

The four-part structure

1. Name the specific gap. Not "I noticed your website could be improved" — say exactly what's missing. "I noticed [Business Name] doesn't show up with a working website when I searched for [service] in [area]" is concrete and impossible to ignore, because it's clearly about them.

2. Show, don't tell. If you've already built a quick demo or mockup, say so and attach or link it. A business owner who can see something beats one who has to imagine it.

3. Make the ask tiny. Not "let's schedule a 30-minute call" — that's a big commitment from a stranger. "Want me to send it over?" or "Worth a 2-minute look?" gets far more replies.

4. Stop. Don't stack three more sentences explaining your services, pricing, or credentials. The first message's only job is to get a reply — everything else comes after.

An example

"Hi [Name], came across [Business Name] while looking at [category] in [area] — noticed there's no website showing up, just the Google listing. I put together a quick demo of what one could look like. Want me to send it over?"

Three sentences. No pricing, no pitch deck, no "I'd love to help you grow your business online." Just a specific observation and a small ask.

Why WhatsApp usually beats email

For local businesses in India, email response rates are low — many small business owners don't check a business inbox regularly, if they have one at all. WhatsApp gets opened. Keep the tone conversational, not like a template, and send from a real number rather than a bulk-messaging tool that gets flagged.

What to do after "yes"

Once they reply with interest, move fast — send the demo, follow up within a day if they go quiet, and have a proposal ready so the conversation doesn't stall waiting on you. A pipeline with clear stages (New → Messaged → Replied → Proposal Sent → Closed) prevents leads from going cold while you're busy with something else.
