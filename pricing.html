Frequently asked

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free plan works immediately after signup — no card required.

What exactly is OpenStreetMap search?

It's a second, independent source of business data — useful for storefronts and offices like cafes, salons, clinics, and shops. It's included on Starter and Business, not on Free, because it pulls from a more resource-intensive data source than the free Auto-search.

What's the difference between Starter and Business?

Starter gets you more leads/month and OpenStreetMap search. Business adds bulk WhatsApp campaigns (message multiple leads in one flow) and 2 team seats for agencies working together.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — both paid plans are billed monthly or yearly with no lock-in. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of your current billing period.
