Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free plan works immediately after signup — no card required.

Is OpenStreetMap search really free?

Yes — it's included on every plan, including Free. It's a second, independent source of business data useful for storefronts and offices like cafes, salons, clinics, and shops.

What do I get by upgrading to Starter?

More monthly leads (1,000 vs. 20) and the instant demo-site generator — the feature that turns a lead into a live preview site to pitch them, in one click.

What's the difference between Starter and Business?

Starter gets you more leads/month and the demo generator. Business adds bulk WhatsApp campaigns (message multiple leads in one flow) and 2 team seats for agencies working together.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — both paid plans are billed monthly or yearly with no lock-in. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of your current billing period.
